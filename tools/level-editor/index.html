      Door gaps, locks, seals and the lair stairs are placed by the game; with Game features on they are drawn as they will appear, outlined in green (doors), amber (lock / seal / crack) and violet (stairs). The mage can be dragged with the Select tool.

      Format 2 lairs: click a room on the grid map to edit it, drag it to move it, Add room then click an empty spot to place a new one. Doors are added on the left (side, segment, a touching room or exit, and the lock); the far side of each door is added by the game and shown dashed. Esc cancels placing a room.
